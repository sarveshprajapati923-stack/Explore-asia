Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

📅 Last Updated: June 1, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to NestAsia ("we", "our", or "us"). NestAsia Technologies Pvt. Ltd. operates the website nestasia.com and related services (the "Platform"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our Platform, how we use it, with whom we share it, and what choices you have. By using NestAsia, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

✅ We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is only used to improve your experience on NestAsia and serve relevant property-related content.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you register or contact us
  • Property details when you post a listing
  • Search preferences and saved properties in your account
  • Messages sent through our contact form or to agents

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on the Platform, and links clicked
  • Search queries, filters used, and properties viewed
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • If you log in via Google or Facebook, we receive your basic profile information
  • We may receive data from analytics providers (Google Analytics) and advertising partners (Google AdSense)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve our property search and listing services
  • To send you property alerts, newsletters and updates you subscribe to
  • To connect you with agents and sellers you enquire about
  • To personalise your property recommendations and search results
  • To process and respond to your contact form submissions
  • To detect, prevent and address fraud or security issues
  • To comply with our legal obligations under applicable law
  • To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense based on your interests

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

NestAsia uses Google AdSense to display advertisements on our Platform. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. This allows Google and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing habits.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

🔒 Google AdSense may use the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads. You can opt out at any time. We do not have access to or control over the cookies used by Google AdSense.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Agents and Sellers: When you submit an enquiry for a property, your contact information is shared with the listing agent or seller to facilitate communication.
  • Service Providers: We work with trusted third parties (hosting providers, analytics tools, email services) who process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6. Cookies Policy

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on NestAsia. Cookies are small data files placed on your device. We use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Platform to function properly (login sessions, search filters)
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our Platform
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your language, location and display preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the Platform.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. Account data is retained for the duration of your account and up to 2 years after closure. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access your personal information we hold
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Right to withdraw consent for email marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at brandtahir806@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

NestAsia is not directed at or intended for use by children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
