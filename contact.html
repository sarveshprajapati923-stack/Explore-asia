Frequently Asked Questions

How do I list my property on NestAsia? +
Click the "Post Free Ad" button on the top right. Fill in your property details, upload photos, and submit. Basic listing is completely free. Your listing will be live within 24 hours after verification.
Is NestAsia free to use for property seekers? +
Yes, completely free. Browsing listings, contacting agents, using EMI calculator, RERA checker, and all other tools are 100% free for buyers and tenants. No hidden charges.
How do I verify a property is RERA registered? +
Use our RERA Checker tool on the homepage. Enter the project RERA registration number and select your state. We cross-check with the state RERA database and show you the status instantly.
How can I report a fake or fraudulent listing? +
Please email us immediately at brandtahir806@gmail.com with the listing URL and reason for concern. We take fraud reports very seriously and investigate within 48 hours. Fraudulent listings are removed immediately.
How can I advertise on NestAsia? +
We offer banner advertising, sponsored property listings, and featured agent profiles. Email us at brandtahir806@gmail.com with subject "Advertising Enquiry" and we will send you our media kit and pricing.
