Who We Are

NestAsia is a trusted online real estate marketplace that helps people buy, sell, and rent properties across India and Asia. Founded with the mission of making property search transparent, safe, and accessible, we serve millions of users every month across India, Singapore, UAE, Thailand, Malaysia, and more.

We believe every person deserves a safe, verified, and stress-free home-buying experience. That is why every listing on NestAsia is verified, every project is RERA-checked, and every agent on our platform is licensed and registered.

What Makes Us Different

RERA Compliance: Every Indian project listed on NestAsia is cross-verified against state RERA databases. We do not allow unregistered or fraudulent projects on our platform.

Verified Agents Only: All agents on NestAsia must submit their RERA agent registration ID or equivalent local license before listing properties. Agents are periodically reviewed for quality and compliance.

No Hidden Charges: Browsing listings, contacting agents, and using our tools (EMI calculator, RERA checker, price trends) are completely free for property seekers.

Multi-Country Coverage: We cover India, Singapore, UAE, Thailand, Malaysia, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nepal, Indonesia, Philippines and more — all in one platform with multi-currency support.
